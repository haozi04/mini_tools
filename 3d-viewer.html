About

A local 3D previewer powered by three.js. Open or drop a model file to inspect it — nothing leaves your device.

Supported formats: .stl (binary & ASCII), .obj (geometry only), .3mf, and .glb / .gltf (embedded geometry). Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, right-drag to pan.
